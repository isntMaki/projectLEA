## Shooting was completely broken — camera was Untagged (13:00)
The client `Player.log` (build of 10:31, so current code) had 12 lines of
`[WeaponUser] No attack origin and no main camera; cannot resolve the hit.` — every single shot
silently failed. Root cause: `FirstPerson Camera` in Manuel.unity was `m_TagString: Untagged`, so
`Camera.main` is null and `WeaponUser.attackOrigin` (resolved once in `Awake`) stayed null
forever. The user read it as "shooting doesn't work, 0/30, reloading doesn't work" — the mag
emptied into nothing.
Fixes:
1. Tagged the camera `MainCamera` in the scene.
2. `WeaponUser` now resolves the origin lazily per shot via an `AttackOrigin` property
   (serialized field -> `Camera.main` -> `GetComponentInChildren<Camera>`), because a network
   client's camera can spawn after `Awake`. Same fallback order added to
   `WeaponViewModel.Socket`.
3. **The viewmodel socket was also null** (`viewModelSocket: {fileID: 0}`), so the gun parented
   to the Player ROOT at a feet-relative offset and never pitched with the view — the "weapon is
   backwards and not in camera view" complaint. Added a `ViewModelSocket` GameObject as a child
   of the camera (fileID 1951002000/1951002001, added to the camera Transform's `m_Children` too)
   and pointed the component at it. The reload dip now moves that socket as designed.

## Bean scale — the axis was the bug all along (13:15)
The bake still reported `Bean is 0.90 m tall -> 1.8 m (scale 2.0043)` after the height constant
change, because the 1.2f edit landed AFTER the 10:28 domain reload — but the constant was never
the problem. `WorldBounds` measures `SkinnedMeshRenderer.localBounds` + `TransformPoint`, and for
an imported Blender FBX those bounds are in the MESH's own Blender space: the `-90deg X` rotation
the export puts on every mesh object is applied at render time, not in those corners. So `size.y`
is Blender's DEPTH (0.90) and the height is `size.z` (1.79). Dividing by the depth gave scale 2.0
and a 3.6 m bean — the "waaay too big" the user asked about repeatedly. (The bean does render
upright: Unity's `RotX(-90deg)` maps local +Z to world +Y, and Blender -Y to world +Z, so the
eyes face +Z — matching the earlier note.)
Fixed `BakeBean` to scale and lift on `size.z` / `min.z` (the old `min.y` lift also floated every
bean 0.84 m), with a warning if Z is not the tallest dimension. `CharacterHeight` is back to 1.8f
— that is what the 2 m CharacterController and the camera at local y=0.8 expect (head at camera
height); the previous 1.2f was a guess. Marker deleted; the bake re-runs on the next reload.
Measured in Blender: Bean height(Z)=1.7921 depth(Y)=0.9503 width(X)=0.9983, feet essentially at
Z=0 (min -0.013).
